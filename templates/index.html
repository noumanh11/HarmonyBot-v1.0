Before you start

HarmonyBot is an AI assistant that can help you think through health and wellbeing questions. It is not a doctor, therapist, or emergency service, and it can be wrong.

  • Do not use it to diagnose or treat a condition.
  • Always confirm anything important with a qualified professional.
  • If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number.

Conversations are stored only in this browser and are never sent anywhere except to generate a reply.
